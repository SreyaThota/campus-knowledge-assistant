# Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
## Academic Curriculum & Degree Program Handbook
**Document Code: M.Sc.-AI-CURRICULUM-2024**
**Issued by: Academic Board of Faculty of Computer Science**

---

### 1. Degree Structure Overview
The Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence is a research-oriented full-time program comprising **120 ECTS credit points** distributed over **4 semesters**.

| Component | ECTS Credits | Requirement Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Core Mandatory Modules (Kernfächer) | 30 ECTS | Compulsory |
| Advanced AI Electives (Vertiefungsfächer) | 35 ECTS | Elective Catalog |
| Practical Course / Research Lab (Praktikum) | 10 ECTS | Practical |
| Interdisciplinary Seminar | 5 ECTS | Seminar |
| General Academic & Soft Skills | 10 ECTS | Interdisciplinary |
| Master's Thesis & Colloquium | 30 ECTS | Capstone |
| **Total** | **120 ECTS** | |

---

### 2. Core Mandatory Modules (30 ECTS)
All students must complete the following four core foundational modules in semesters 1 and 2:
1. **IN2064: Advanced Machine Learning (8 ECTS)**
   - Content: Statistical learning theory, Gaussian processes, Bayesian inference, regularized empirical risk minimization.
   - Examination: Written exam (120 minutes) at the end of the winter semester.
2. **IN2346: Deep Learning & Neural Architectures (8 ECTS)**
   - Content: Transformers, Attention mechanisms, Convolutional networks, Diffusion models, and Generative Adversarial Networks.
   - Prerequisite: Linear Algebra and Python programming proficiency.
3. **IN2089: Vector Databases & Neural Information Retrieval (6 ECTS)**
   - Content: Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search, HNSW graphs, Dense Vector Embeddings, RAG architectures, and Hybrid Search (BM25 + Dense).
   - Practical coursework: Implementation of a scalable RAG indexing pipeline.
4. **IN2155: AI Ethics, Safety, and EU AI Act Compliance (8 ECTS)**
   - Content: Explainable AI (XAI), algorithmic fairness, data privacy, GDPR implications in machine learning, and high-risk AI classification under the European Union AI Act.

---

### 3. Practical Research Lab (10 ECTS)
- The practical lab (Praktikum) involves hands-on development in teams of 2 to 4 students under the supervision of a chair lab.
- Students must submit a production-ready codebase, containerized Docker deployment, automated test suite, and a 15-page technical report.
- Prerequisites: Completion of at least two Core Modules.

---

### 4. Progression & Academic Standing Milestones
- **End of Semester 2 Milestone:** Students must have successfully accumulated at least **30 ECTS**. If fewer than 30 ECTS are attained, a mandatory academic advisory consultation (Studienberatung) is triggered.
- **End of Semester 3 Milestone:** Students must have accumulated at least **60 ECTS** to be eligible for registration of the Master's Thesis (§ 35 FPSO).
- **Maximum Duration:** The maximum period permitted to complete all 120 ECTS is **six semesters**. An extension to the seventh semester requires formal approval by the Examination Board (Prüfungsausschuss).

---

### 5. Recognition of Prior Learning & Industrial Internships
- An industrial Werkstudent position or internship cannot directly substitute for academic modules or the Master's Thesis.
- However, an external Master's Thesis conducted in collaboration with an industrial partner (e.g., Siemens, BMW, Celonis, or SAP) is permitted, provided a university professor acts as the official primary academic supervisor (Erstprüfer).
