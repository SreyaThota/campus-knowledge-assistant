UNIVERSITY CENTRAL LIBRARY (UNIVERSITÄTSBIBLIOTHEK)
USER GUIDELINES, CIRCULATION POLICY & OPENING HOURS
Document Code: UB-REG-2024-V2
Applicable to: All enrolled students, faculty, and registered visiting researchers.

1. OPERATING HOURS & BUILDING ACCESS
- Main Campus Central Library:
  Monday through Friday: 08:00 - 24:00 (Midnight)
  Saturday and Sunday: 09:00 - 22:00
  Public Holidays: Closed (Notice posted on online portal 14 days in advance)
- Computer Lab & Terminal Center: 24/7 access with validated Student Card (CampusCard RFID).
- Information Desk & Circulation Counter:
  Monday through Friday: 09:00 - 18:00
  Self-checkout kiosks (Selbstverbuchungsstationen) operate at all hours when the building is open.

2. STUDY ZONES & HOUSE RULES
- Silent Study Halls (Großer Lesesaal):
  Strict silence must be maintained at all times. Mobile phones must be set to silent mode. Phone calls, audio playback without headphones, and whispering are strictly prohibited.
- Refreshments & Food Policy:
  Eating food is prohibited in all study and computer areas. Only beverages in completely leak-proof, transparent, sealable bottles are permitted. Coffee cups with disposable lids or open cans are strictly forbidden.
- Group Study Rooms (Gruppenarbeitsräume):
  Rooms 101 to 115 are designated for collaborative academic work. Rooms can be booked via the online Library Booking Portal up to 7 calendar days in advance for groups of 3 to 8 students. Maximum booking duration is 3 consecutive hours per group per day.
- Lockers and Cloakroom:
  Backpacks and large bags must be stored in the electronic lockers in the foyer. Lockers operate with the student card. Lockers are reset automatically every night at 02:00 AM; items left overnight are collected by campus security and stored in Lost & Found for a €10 retrieval administrative fee.

3. BORROWING PRIVILEGES & LOAN PERIODS
- Standard Collection:
  Enrolled students may borrow up to 25 physical books simultaneously.
  Standard loan duration: 28 calendar days.
  Automatic renewals: Items are automatically renewed up to 3 times (each for 28 days), provided no reservation (Vormerkung) has been placed by another patron.
- Short-Loan & Semester Reserve Collection (Semesterapparat):
  Red-labeled books may only be used within the library premises during daytime hours. Overnight loans are permitted from 19:00 until 10:00 the following business day.
- Overdue Fines and Penalties:
  First week overdue: €1.50 per book per week.
  Second reminder: €5.00 administrative fee plus accumulated daily rate.
  Third reminder: Borrowing privileges are suspended campus-wide until all outstanding items are returned and balances cleared.

4. INTER-LIBRARY LOANS & DIGITAL REPOSITORY
- Digital access: All students have remote off-campus access to SpringerLink, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, and Elsevier ScienceDirect via institutional VPN / eduGAIN Shibboleth proxy.
- Inter-library loan (Fernleihe): Books not available locally can be requested through Bavarian inter-library loan (BVB) for a subsidized processing fee of €1.50 per requested title. Delivery time is typically 5 to 10 business days.
