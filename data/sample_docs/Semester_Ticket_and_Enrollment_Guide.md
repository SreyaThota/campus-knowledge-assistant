# Campus Student Services & Registrar's Office
## Enrollment, Semester Ticket, and Administrative Procedures
**Document Code: STU-ADM-REG-2024**
**Issued: Academic Year 2024/2025**

---

### 1. Semester Re-Registration (Rückmeldung) Deadlines
All continuing students must re-register for each subsequent semester by transferring the semester fees in full:
- **Summer Semester Re-Registration:** January 15 to **February 15** (strictly enforced).
- **Winter Semester Re-Registration:** June 15 to **August 15** (strictly enforced).
- Late Re-Registration Fee: If the payment is received after the deadline, an administrative penalty fee of **€25.00** is assessed automatically.
- Exmatriculation Warning: Failure to pay before the second reminder deadline results in automatic exmatriculation (Zwangsexmatrikulation) at the end of the current semester.

---

### 2. Breakdown of Semester Fees (Semesterbeitrag)
The standard semester contribution is **€85.00 per semester**, composed of:
1. **Student Union Fee (Studentenwerksbeitrag):** €75.00 (subsidizes student dining halls/Mensa, psychological counseling, and student housing).
2. **Solidarity Fee (Solidaritätsbeitrag für Grundnetz):** €10.00.

Payment details:
- Must be transferred via SEPA bank transfer to the university treasury account.
- The reference field (Verwendungszweck) must state: `RUECK-[Student_ID_Number]-[Surname]`.

---

### 3. Public Transportation & Deutschlandticket Upgrade
- **Basic Campus Ticket (Grundticket):**
  Included in the student registration. Valid on public transit (U-Bahn, S-Bahn, Tram, Bus) within network zones M-6 only during off-peak hours:
  - Monday to Friday: 18:00 to 06:00 the following morning.
  - Saturday, Sunday, and official Bavarian public holidays: All day (24 hours).
- **Deutschlandticket / 29-Euro-Ticket Upgrade:**
  - Enrolled students are eligible for the subsidized Bavarian Student Deutschlandticket at a price of **€29.00 per month**.
  - Provides unlimited second-class regional rail (RE, RB) and local public transit access across all of Germany.
  - Subscription is managed digitally via the regional transit operator app (MVG Fahrinfo / DB Navigator) after authentication via university SSO (Shibboleth).
  - Cancellation deadline: Must be cancelled by the 10th of the current calendar month to terminate at month-end.

---

### 4. Student Card Validation (CampusCard)
- The physical student card contains a thermo-rewriteable strip that displays the valid semester dates.
- After successful fee payment (usually 3 business days after bank transfer), students must insert their card into one of the blue **Card Validation Terminals (Validierungsstationen)** located in the main foyer (Building 01, Room 00.02.001) or the Informatics building (Garching-Forschungszentrum).
- A card that has not been re-validated is not recognized as a valid transit ticket during ticket inspections (Fahrkartenkontrolle).
